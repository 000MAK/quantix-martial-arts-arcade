Research agenda

Analytical dimensions

01

Computer vision

Pose features, joint tracking, limb velocity, and spatiotemporal kinematics extracted from video.

02

State transitions

Stochastic transitions, position dwell time, and semi-Markov summaries that describe flow and pressure.

03

Acoustic analysis

Impact signatures and environmental signal decomposition for strikes, contact, and training context.

04

Injury modeling

Actuarial and survival-oriented analyses to understand exposure, risk, and recovery patterns.

05

Action patterns

Tactical analysis of decision sequences, position control, and outcome-relevant action structures.
